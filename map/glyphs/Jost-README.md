# Jost (Deep space basemap, round 164s)

Jost by The Jost Project Authors (indestructible type), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(Jost-OFL.txt), from github.com/google/fonts (ofl/jost).

- `Jost-Light/`, `Jost-Regular/`: Jost at weights 300 and 400,
- `Jost-LightItalic/`: Jost Italic at weight 300,

cut into MapLibre's letter files (signed distance fields, 24 px, buffer 3,
radius 8, cutoff 0.25), ranges 0-8959 and 65280-65535, for the Deep space
basemap's place names. Jost covers Latin, Latin Extended and Cyrillic; it has
no Greek letters.
